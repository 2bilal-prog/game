MOON DASH 2P

Race across the Moon side by side. Same course, same hazards. The higher score wins, and the last runner standing keeps scoring.

PLAYER 1, Sky Hero: press Space to jump, then hold it in the air to fly with the jetpack. One life.

PLAYER 2, Brute: ↑ jumps (press again for a double jump), ↓ ducks. Smashes through obstacles, but each hit costs 1 of 2 hearts.

Phone: in 2 player mode tap the left half for Player 1 and the right half for Player 2 (bottom strip to duck). Water and craters are instant defeat for both.
