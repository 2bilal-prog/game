HERO DASH 2P

Race through the city side by side. Same course, same hazards. The higher score wins, and the last runner standing keeps scoring.

PLAYER 1: Space jumps (flyers hold it in the air to fly), F uses your special ability.

PLAYER 2: ↑ jumps (double jump on foot), ↓ ducks, Enter uses your ability. Runners can take hits, flyers can’t. Phone: tap the top of your half for your ability.

THE MONSTER: A hungry moon monster is chasing you from behind. Crystals shove it back, and every hit you take lets it close in. If it catches you, you get eaten.

Phone: in 2 player mode tap the left half for Player 1 and the right half for Player 2 (bottom strip to duck). Water and craters are instant defeat for both.
