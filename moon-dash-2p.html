GOKULDHAM BATTLE ROYALE

14 fighters drop in. The storm closes in. Last hero standing says BOOYAH!

Tap a photo to choose your fighter. Use the dropdown under each photo to set which hero it is (it decides the power).

PC: WASD move, mouse aim and click to shoot, E hero power. Phone: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires, tap the glowing button for your power. Pick up guns, medkits and armor.
